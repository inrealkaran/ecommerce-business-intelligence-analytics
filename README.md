# 🛒 E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end e-commerce data analytics project using the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset. The goal is to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, seller performance, and delivery operations to uncover actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Analyze revenue and order trends over time.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Evaluate delivery performance and its relationship with customer satisfaction.
- Understand customer retention and repeat purchasing behavior.
- Analyze seller contribution and payment preferences.
- Generate data-driven business recommendations.

## 🗂️ Dataset
The project uses the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset, which contains multiple datasets covering orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, reviews, and geolocation.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Monthly revenue analysis
- Product category revenue analysis
- Category order volume analysis
- Delivery performance analysis
- Delivery delays vs. customer review scores
- Late delivery analysis by product category
- Payment method analysis
- Seller revenue and concentration analysis
- Customer repeat purchase analysis
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Monthly order trends

## 💡 Key Findings
- Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue among product categories.
- Bed, Bath & Table recorded the highest order count among categories.
- Approximately 93.23% of delivered orders arrived on time or early.
- Late deliveries were associated with significantly lower review scores.
- Repeat customers represented approximately 3% of identified customers.
- Top 10 sellers contributed approximately 13.15% of total revenue.
- Average Order Value was R$137.75, while median order value was R$86.90.

## 📈 Business Recommendations
- Improve customer retention through loyalty programs and targeted campaigns.
- Monitor late deliveries and identify operational bottlenecks.
- Prioritize delayed orders to improve customer satisfaction.
- Develop category-specific sales strategies.
- Encourage repeat purchases through personalized recommendations.
